The challenge

More and more video is made by AI agents that write code: they lay out scenes, set type, choose colors and animate transitions in code, then render the result. HeyGen needed a way to tell which models actually do this well.

Most code benchmarks check whether a program runs or whether its output matches a target. Motion design is different. A video can render perfectly and still look wrong: off-brand type, cluttered layout, or pacing that drags. What matters is temporal aesthetics: what changes over time, and whether the motion feels intentional.

Building Code2Video

Code2Video is a benchmark for code-driven motion design. Given a brief, an agent must produce a rendered motion-graphics sequence that follows the requested visual direction: composition, typography, colors, timing, animation, transitions and motion behavior.

The benchmark has 168 human-written briefs. Each one is a beat of a real product-launch video:

  • Hook
  • Problem statement
  • Product intro
  • Key features
  • Benefits
  • Social proof
  • Call to action
  • Brand outro

Every brief has a human-made reference, so the question isn’t just “is this good?” but “is this as good as what a skilled designer made?”

A judge trained on people

Scoring video at scale needs an automated judge, and off-the-shelf vision-language models weren’t reliable enough. HeyGen built its own evaluation model, a dedicated architecture rather than a fine-tuned LLM or VLM, trained on human preference data to predict which of two videos a person would prefer.

The judge compares two videos made from the same brief across five dimensions:

boltEngagement
targetPrompt intent
dashboardComposition

Launching with Kaggle

A benchmark only stays useful if it keeps up with new models. HeyGen launched Code2Video on Kaggle on September 18, 2026, and Kaggle maintains the leaderboard, adding new models as they launch.

visibilityTransparent

Methodology, briefs and scores are public on the leaderboard.

hubComprehensive

New frontier models are ranked as they ship, with no extra work for HeyGen.

replayReproducible

Versioned tasks mean any result can be re-run and checked.
